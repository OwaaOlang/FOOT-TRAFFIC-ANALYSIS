FINDINGS

1. The revenue drop was concentrated in one customer.
Total revenue fell from August to September. Mukasa Dairy alone dropped from 22,932,500 UGX to 9,767,500 UGX, a loss of 13,165,000 UGX (-57.4%). This one customer accounts for more than half of the total business drop. Small customers like Mama Naki (+18.7%) and Nansana Mart (+12.9%) actually grew, so this was not a market-wide collapse.

2. Yoghurt, not Ghee, was the product that collapsed.
For Mukasa Dairy:
- Yoghurt: 11,963,500 UGX → 3,782,000 UGX (-8,181,500 / -68%)
- Ghee: 7,058,000 UGX → 3,899,000 UGX (-45%)
- Fresh Milk: 3,911,000 UGX → 2,006,500 UGX (-49%)
Yoghurt alone was 62% of Mukasa's total loss. In August, Mukasa + Yoghurt was the single biggest customer-product combination in the business.

3. The loss was caused by a price cut combined with lower volume.
Mukasa's average Yoghurt price was cut from 9,380 UGX to 4,115 UGX (-56%). Ghee was cut from 7,938 UGX to 5,893 UGX (-26%) and Fresh Milk from 5,269 UGX to 4,700 UGX (-11%). Even with much lower prices, Mukasa still bought less. The discount did not work - the business earned less per unit and sold fewer units.
